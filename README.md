# We-Code-You-Play_Be-a-Bee
This is a simulation of honey bee harvesting game which is inspired by the process of honey bee harvesting on a daily basis. In the game you become the bee and pollen is the goal. In the first level it is a simple collection of some pollen; then you reach the second level of lightning and collect more pollen; in reaching the third level, you will encounter more challenges in the process of collecting pollen. In the final challenge you will encounter life-threatening natural disasters such as rain, utility pole bushes, pesticides, diseases, etc. When you touch them your blood bar will drop until you die. If you manage to collect all the pollen you can win you win, otherwise you lose.
